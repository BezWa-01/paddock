Controls

Space plays and pauses. Left and right arrows step a beat. M mutes and unmutes. Click or drag the bar to scrub — the picture redraws to match wherever you land. The speaker button shows what the sound is doing now: “Sound on” means you can hear it, “Muted” means it is playing silently.

The voice and the speed

The narration is a rendered audio track, not your browser talking — the same on every device, and it keeps working once the page has loaded even with no signal. Pick a voice under the player: two Australian, two American. Each loads the first time you choose it, and the page keeps your place when you switch. The speed buttons run it from 0.85× to 2× without changing the pitch, and both choices are remembered. At 1.5× the whole thing takes about two-thirds of the time shown.
